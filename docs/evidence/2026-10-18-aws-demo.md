The three local Compose containers were matched by their project labels and stopped; PostgreSQL
exited successfully and its volumes were preserved. No other local services were changed.

The bootstrap state bucket is the only retained billable project resource identified by this
inventory. A secret [marked for deletion](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/secretsmanager/latest/userguide/manage_delete-secret.html)
is not charged during its recovery window, but its name is unavailable for immediate reuse.
These checks cover this demonstration's owned resources;
they do not claim that unrelated resources in the AWS account are absent or that the final bill
is zero.

The cleanup preflight rejected an initial plan that included unrelated RDS parameter and ECS
task-definition changes. AWS had returned explicit defaults omitted by the configuration. The
configuration now records the parameter's apply method, the volume's launch setting, and empty ECS
lists explicitly. A fresh live plan then showed zero creates, only the three intended deletion-guard
updates, and zero deletes. The unexpected plan was not applied.
